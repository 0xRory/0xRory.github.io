[10] · Security · 2025

Unphishable

Next.jsTypeScriptWeb3

Unphishable is an interactive Web3 anti-phishing training platform — 30+ gamified challenges covering seed-phrase scams, malicious token approvals, fake airdrops, clipboard hijacking, and punycode look-alike domains, in English and Traditional/Simplified Chinese. Built as a public good: free for anyone to use.

Core team: DeFiHackLabs, ScamSniffer, and SlowMist, with support from the Ethereum Ecosystem Support Program. I contribute through DeFiHackLabs, the same Web3 security co-learning community behind the Solidity 21-day program listed under Writing.
